VoxPilot Assistive System Guide

About this software: This program converts vocal frequency (pitch) or intensity (volume) into continuous flight lift to help patients with speech disorders (dysarthria, apraxia, LSVT) practice vocal modulation safely and independently.

🎤 Voice Control Mode: Guide the bird by humming or projecting inside the target range highlighted in the spectrogram on the left. The voice analysis has an integrated noise filter; whispers do not generate lift, which encourages voicing.

⌨️ Keyboard Control Mode: Switch to Keyboard Mode to fly with the Spacebar or Up Arrow. Each tap flaps the wings and gives an instant arcade-style jump upward, similar to Flappy Bird. Clinical vocal history trackers remain active in the sidebar for diagnostic feedback.
